The Stoics didn't give rules. | They gave pictures. Here are five you won't forget.
One. The bull and the lion. A bull finds out how strong it is when the lion shows up. Trouble doesn't break you. It introduces you.
Two. The rock in the sea. Waves hit it all day. It doesn't argue back. It just stands there, and the water calms down.
Three. The old man's tree. He plants a tree he'll never sit under. Do work that pays off after you're gone.
Four. The shaken bowl. When the water shakes, the sun's reflection breaks. But the sun is fine. Calm the water, not the sky.
Five. The archer. He controls the aim. Not the wind. | Which one do you need today? Drop the number.
