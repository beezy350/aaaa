Silk Road Wisdom. The last part. | At dawn, the hunter came over the hill.
Only then did the mouse bite the last rope.
The cat leapt into a tree. The mouse dove into his hole. | The hunter found an empty net.
Days later, the cat called sweetly. Come out, my friend. I owe you my life.
From the dark, the mouse answered. We were friends because we needed each other. That need is gone.
Help your enemy when you must. | Never trust him after.
