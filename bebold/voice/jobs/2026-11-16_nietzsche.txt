Nietzsche thought the Stoics were fooling themselves. | Was he right? Let's look at both sides.
First, the Stoic claim. Live in agreement with nature. Nature is rational, so be rational too.
Then Nietzsche's attack. Nature is wasteful and indifferent, he wrote. You're not reading nature's rules. You're painting your own rules onto it.
His deeper point? Every philosophy builds the world in its own image. The Stoics did too.
A modern Stoic might answer: fine. Call it a choice, not a law of nature. Choosing calm still beats getting thrown around by every storm.
So you decide. | Nietzsche, or the Stoics? Tell me in the comments.
