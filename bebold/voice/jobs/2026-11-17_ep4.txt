Silk Road Wisdom. Episode four. A new story. | The pond was drying up. And a tortoise was about to lose his home.
His two friends, a pair of geese, had a plan. Bite this stick, they said. We'll fly you to a new lake.
But there was one rule. Don't open your mouth. Not once. Not for anything.
And they flew. A tortoise in the sky. It was actually working.
Then the village below looked up. People pointed. They laughed. They shouted at him.
And the tortoise? He had something to say. | What would you have done? Follow for part five.
