In Japan, when a bowl breaks, some craftsmen do not hide the crack.
They fill it with lacquer and gold.
It is called kintsugi.
The story goes that a shogun sent a broken tea bowl to China for repair.
It came back held together with ugly metal staples.
So Japanese craftsmen searched for a better way.
The break became part of the bowl's story.
Your cracks are not the end of you. They are where the gold goes.
