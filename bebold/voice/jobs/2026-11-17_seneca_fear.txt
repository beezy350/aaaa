Planning for the future, or just worrying about it? | Here's Seneca's test. He wrote it to a friend who was scared of what was coming.
One. We suffer more in imagination than in reality. More things scare us than actually hurt us.
Two. Ask yourself: is this happening right now? If not, it's a story. Treat it like one.
Three. Planning has a pen. Worry has a loop. If you're writing steps, you're planning. If you're replaying fears, stop.
Four. Look at the worst case once. Squarely. Prepare for it. Then stop rehearsing it.
Five. Don't be miserable before the crisis shows up. It may never come. | Save this for your next three a.m. spiral.
