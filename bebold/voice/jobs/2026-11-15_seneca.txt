Rich, but feeling empty? | Seneca knew that feeling. He was one of the richest men in Rome. Here are his five fixes.
One. Poor isn't having too little. Poor is always wanting more.
Two. Practice being poor. A few days of plain bread and a rough cloak, and the fear of losing it all shrinks fast.
Three. Time is the real money. It's the only thing that's truly yours. So spend it like gold.
Four. Make money your servant. For the wise, wealth serves. For the fool, it rules.
Five. Pick a port. No wind helps a sailor who doesn't know where he's going. Drive follows direction. | Which one do you need? Comment the number.
