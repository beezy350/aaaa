Arkad's son lost his father's gold on a rigged horse race.
Then he lost more to a bad partner.
But his father had given him a clay tablet carved with five laws.
The second law said this.
Gold labors diligently and contentedly for the wise owner who finds it profitable work.
Money sitting still does nothing.
Money put to wise work multiplies, like flocks in the field.
So ask yourself. Is your gold working, or sleeping?
