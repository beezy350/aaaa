Silk Road Wisdom. Episode five. | The whole village was shouting at the sky.
Look at that fool, they laughed. A tortoise who thinks he can fly.
He held on. He bit down hard. But every word stung.
Then he opened his mouth, to answer them.
And the sky let him go.
The geese flew on. The insult cost him nothing. His answer cost him everything. | Some words aren't worth the fall. Comment one thing you'll never answer back.
