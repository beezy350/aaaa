Silk Road Wisdom, episode one. A crow begged a mouse to be his friend. The mouse had every reason to say no.
The crow had watched the mouse free the doves from the hunter's net. He wanted a friend like that.
But the mouse said no. Crows eat mice. A friendship with your hunter is a trap.
So the crow refused to leave. He stayed at the door, until his patience spoke louder than his beak.
Trust was earned, not asked for. Soon, a tortoise and a gazelle joined them.
Then the hunter came back. And this time, his net caught one of them. Follow for part two.
