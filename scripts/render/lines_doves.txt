A flock of doves stole a hunter's net. | While they were trapped inside it.
They landed for free grain. Then the net dropped.
Every dove pulled its own way. And the harder they fought alone, the tighter it held.
Then their leader said: pull together. | They rose as one. And the net rose with them.
A mouse chewed them free. But the leader made him free the others first. Herself, last.
Alone, it's a trap. Together, it's cargo. | Who would you lift the net with?
