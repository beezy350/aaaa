Marcus Aurelius on losing your job to AI. He ruled Rome through plague and war, and his notes still work.
One. The machine isn't the pain. He wrote that what hurts is our judgment of a thing, not the thing itself. Change the story first.
Two. Meet tomorrow with today's tools. The future gets the same clear head you're using right now. Fear only borrows trouble.
Three. You are not your title. Even as emperor, he warned himself not to let the role swallow the man.
Four. Do the work only you can do. His morning rule: get up, and do the work of a human being. Machines copy tasks. Not character.
Five. Change is the rule. Nature keeps turning old things into new ones. Your job will change. So can you. Which one helps you most? Comment the number.
