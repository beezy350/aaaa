Silk Road Wisdom. A new story. | A monkey lived in a fig tree by the sea.
Every day, he dropped sweet figs to a tortoise below. | They became friends.
But the tortoise's wife was jealous. | She faked an illness, and said only a monkey's heart could cure her.
So the tortoise invited the monkey home, on his back, across the water.
Halfway out, he confessed. | My wife wants your heart.
The monkey stayed calm. | My heart? I left it in the tree. Take me back, and I'll get it.
Back on shore, he leapt into the tree, and never came down. | In danger, keep your head.
