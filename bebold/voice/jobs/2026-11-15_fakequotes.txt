Three Stoic quotes you've probably shared. | Only one of them is real.
Fake number one. The lion doesn't concern himself with the opinion of the sheep. That's not Marcus Aurelius. It's Tywin Lannister, from Game of Thrones.
Fake number two. Be silent, and let your success make the noise. Nobody has ever found an ancient source for it. Not one.
And the real one. Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one. That's Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, book ten.
Bonus myth. A real Stoic feels nothing. Wrong. The Stoics wanted joy. They just didn't want to be ruled by fear and anger.
