Silk Road Wisdom. Episode seven. | The mouse walked right up to the cat.
I'll chew through your net, he said. But first, you protect me.
The cat agreed. The mouse slipped under his paw, and the weasel and the owl gave up and left.
Then the mouse began to chew. Slowly. Very slowly.
Hurry, the cat hissed. But the mouse kept one rope uncut.
At dawn, footsteps. The hunter was coming. | Why was the mouse waiting? Follow for the finale.
