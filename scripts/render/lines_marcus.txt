Losing your job to AI? | Marcus Aurelius has something for you. He ran Rome through a plague and a war. And he wrote it all down.
Number one. The machine isn't what hurts. What hurts is the story you tell yourself about it. So change the story first.
Number two. Meet tomorrow with the same clear head you've got right now. Fear just borrows trouble.
Number three. You are not your job title. Even as emperor, he kept warning himself: don't let the role swallow the man.
Number four. Do the work only you can do. Every morning, he'd tell himself: get up. Do the work of a human being. Machines copy tasks. They can't copy character.
And number five. Change is the rule. Nature keeps turning old things into new ones. Your job will change. So can you. Which one hit home? Drop the number below.
