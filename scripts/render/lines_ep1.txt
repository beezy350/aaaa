Silk Road Wisdom. Episode one. | A crow begged a mouse to be his friend. And the mouse? He had every reason to say no.
See, the crow had watched this mouse free a flock of doves from a hunter's net. And he thought, I want a friend like that.
But the mouse said no. Crows eat mice. Making friends with your hunter? That's a trap.
So the crow just… stayed. Right outside his door. Until his patience spoke louder than his beak.
Trust was earned. Not asked for. And soon, a tortoise and a gazelle joined them.
Then the hunter came back. And this time, his net caught one of them. Follow for part two.
